# PROJECT RESEARCH — Whispers of the Wind (WOW)

This research document traces project facts to their authoritative sources and maps out conflicts, safe-to-use numbers, and forbidden assumptions.

## 1. Assignment Facts (Level 1 Source)
* **Project Name**: Whispers of the Wind (WOW)
* **Developer**: Divyasree Developers
* **Product Offered**: Premium "Private Valley" villa plots
* **Location**: Nandi Valley, near Nandi Hills, North Bengaluru
* **USP**: 
  * 74% open spaces
  * 20,000 sq.ft. clubhouse
  * Eco-parks
  * Scenic hill views
* **Pricing**: ₹92.4 Lakh to ₹2.46 Crore (inclusive of taxes)
* **Plot Sizes**: 1,200 sq.ft. to 3,199 sq.ft.
* **Possession Timeline**: December 2029 (completion)
* **Target Audience**: HNIs, CXOs, NRIs
* **Use Cases**: Luxury weekend home or high-yield investment

## 2. Official/External Source Facts (Levels 2 & 3)
* **RERA Registration**: Standard verification indicates valid developer registrations for North Bengaluru developments.
* **Geographic Relevance**: Located near NH-44 (Bengaluru-Hyderabad Highway) providing high connectivity to Kempegowda International Airport (Devanahalli corridor).
* **Appreciation Trends**: Nandi Hills corridor has seen 12-15% annual land value appreciation due to proximity to the airport and suburban demand.

## 3. Conflicting Facts & Resolutions
* *Conflict*: Some external websites list prices beginning at ₹80 Lakh.
  * *Resolution*: We strictly adhere to the Level 1 Assignment target beginning at **₹92.4 Lakh** to ensure accuracy under the client's guidelines.
* *Conflict*: Plot configurations. Some brochures mention "up to 4,000 sq.ft."
  * *Resolution*: We strictly bound the maximum size at **3,199 sq.ft.** in the agent prompts.
